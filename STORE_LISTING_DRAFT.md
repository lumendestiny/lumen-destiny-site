## Full description draft — EN

Lumen Destiny is a fortune and lifestyle experience designed to make Four Pillars and compatibility readings easier to explore.

Enter the information needed for a reading, such as birth date and birth time, to view your Four Pillars result and interpretation. You can also explore compatibility results based on information for two people.

LUMEN GUARDIAN lets you browse guardian artwork and wish themes, personalize a preview with a display name and wish, and explore a gifting experience.

Core V1 reading features are available for free. Face-photo reading and 1:1 AI consultation are not part of the public V1 scope.

Lumen Destiny results are provided for self-reflection and entertainment and are not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or other expert advice.
